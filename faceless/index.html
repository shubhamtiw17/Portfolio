faceless-pipeline

A private, single-user publishing tool by Shubham Tiwari

▶ Uses YouTube API Services

faceless-pipeline is an internal tool that uploads videos I produce to my own YouTube channels, Cash & Court and The Rich Loophole. It sets the title, description, tags and thumbnail, and can schedule a publishing time.

What it does

  • Uploads the owner's videos with the YouTube Data API (videos.insert, thumbnails.set).
  • Only the channel owner can authorise it (Google OAuth). It is not offered to other users.
  • It does not read, store or share any data about viewers or other YouTube users.

Content

The channels publish narrated documentaries about public court cases and finance topics. Every video lists its sources (public articles and the court records and news reports they cite). Narration uses an AI voice and visuals are AI-generated illustrations; this is disclosed in each description.
